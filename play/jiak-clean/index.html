Jiak Clean

A working prototype of a household food-rescue app. It makes the next right action with your food fast, positive and local, and works even when your kitchen list is incomplete.

New after the persona playtest

  • Less to read. Shorter copy on every screen. Impact is split into This week, Progress and Community tabs, and demo-only settings fold away.
  • Food that looks like food. Every recipe is now a plated dish with rice grains, egg curds, tomato wedges, glossy kai lan, prawns, congee, a claypot and more, with steam rising. Fridge foods have light and shading.
  • Learn & donate. Flashcards with spaced review, a timed fridge-sort game and a use-by or best-before game. First correct answers each day earn points that a sponsor turns into meals for a food charity you choose (demo, no real money).
  • Charts that move. Bars grow, numbers count up, rings draw, the thermometer and rice cups fill, and garden plants sway and sprout. Motion switches off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Research-backed fixes. Colour-coded fridge zones and a door map, a fridge temperature check, a fullness meter, food hidden at the back, guided put-away with older food in front, a rice planner, a look-smell-taste check for best-before food, an eat-down week, dated tub labels, weekly waste feedback and community fridges.
  • See your fridge. A live, drawn view of the fridge, freezer, cupboard and counter. Bananas brown, greens wilt and frozen food frosts over as the days pass.
  • Leaves the app. Lock-screen widget, fridge kit with a tap-to-log magnet, chat helper and one prompt at a moment you choose.
  • Less typing. Supermarket receipts import themselves and a weekly fridge snap corrects the list.
  • Fits real homes. Mandarin, Malay and Tamil in Simple mode; Tagalog and Bahasa Indonesia for helpers; teen, helper, flatmate and caregiver roles; eating-out mode.
  • Teaches in seconds. Facts as you log, myth or fact, and why-food-goes-off stories for kids.
  • Something new each week. Kitchen garden, mystery basket, festive weeks, rotating missions, teen squads, points that become donated meals.
  • Basics fixed. Today folds to about 1.5 screens, no text under 13 px, no neighbour sharing under 18.

Sample data: foods, prices, people, neighbours, partners and the helpline number are invented. Progress is kept in this browser only. Not legal or medical advice.
