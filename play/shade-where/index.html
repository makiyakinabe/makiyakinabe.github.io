        Your trips stay on your device

        Everything shade,where? does with your trip happens inside your browser. Your searches, start and end points and times are never sent anywhere, and there are no accounts, cookies, ads or tracking.

        ✓ What stays here

        • What you type in search: the full address list downloads once and is searched on your device.
        • Your start, end, time and route: worked out in your browser.
        • Your last trip and route option, saved in this browser only, so the page remembers them.

        ✕ What we never do

        • No sign-in, no cookies, no analytics, no ads.
        • No location tracking: the page never asks for GPS.
        • No third parties: fonts, map library and all map data load from this same site.

        What the site itself sees

        Like any website, the host sends the page and its files. Map data comes in 2 km squares for the area you look at, so the host can tell which squares were loaded, but never your searches, your exact points or your route.
