# Hawker Hero 小贩英雄

A Singapore hawker-centre cooking game. Take over Uncle Lim's chicken rice stall, cook, serve and keep Bahagia Food Centre open.

**Play:** https://makiyakinabe.github.io/hawker-hero/

- 19 hawker stalls, each with its own dishes and mentor: Katong laksa, fried carrot cake, bak kut teh and chendol are the newest
- Every mentor has a four-part story plus a two-part second chapter that unlocks after their recipe
- Five-chapter main story
- Five roles: boss, chef, counter crew, kitchen helper, cleaner
- Age groups: Little Hawker (5–8), Young Hawker (9–12), Hawker Hero (13+)
- Guided first tries, live queue, food that keeps cooking, regulars, a full hawker day
- Works on phones, tablets and desktops, in portrait or landscape

The whole game is one self-contained `index.html` (no build step, no server). `hawker-hero-intro.mp4` is the intro film.

### New in this update (from the gameplay review)
- Every shift starts with your role and today's goals, and the receipt ticks them off
- The closing receipt shows the full cost of running a stall: rent, gas and electricity, packaging, market spending and waste. Tap any line to learn what it means
- Customers have personalities (office worker, student, elderly regular, tourist, family, food lover) that change their patience, orders and tips
- Some customers ask questions; pick the best answer to keep them happy and learn something
- Hawker Book: collect dish cards and hawker words (chope, dabao, kopi-o kosong, wok hei…) plus 12 achievements
- Surprise rush events: a sudden crowd, a visiting food blogger, a neighbour lending a hand
- Six career stages from Apprentice to Mentor
- Settings: Relaxed / Standard / Challenge pace, bigger text and calm motion

### Hawker-life update
- Choose your hawker identity: the Perfectionist, the Inventive Young Hawker, a Third-Generation Family Stall or a Late-Night Supper Stall. Each one changes who comes and what pays off
- Name a signature dish; good shifts with it spread the word
- A Hawker's Choice every other day: follow a trend or keep the recipe, raise prices or absorb costs, join a delivery app, take in an apprentice and more
- Customer reviews after every service, plus a chance of going viral
- New neighbourhood events: community clean-up, school holidays, exam results day, supper crowd; after-school students at dinner
- Hawker Book: Heritage cards (one a day) and a Hawker Trail of real places to try each dish
- My Hawker Journey: a shareable card, plus a visit link that lets friends see your stall

### Living hawker centre update
- Centre Board: a shared centre rating, a daily centre issue you can help with, set meals with neighbouring stalls, a weekly challenge, Hawker of the Week awards (customer love, consistency, community, sustainability) and a centre committee vote from Business Owner rank
- Green upgrades and an eco score; food-waste sorting and the committee's digester cut spoilage
- Festivals every 7th day (Chinese New Year, Hari Raya, Deepavali, National Day, Mid-Autumn) and MRT disruptions
- A comeback plan when a stall's reputation falls or you lose money two days running
- Hawker Academy: lessons and quizzes on menu costing, food safety, customer service and going digital
- Pass a recipe on to an apprentice at Mentor rank
- Colour-blind friendly patience bars

### "Living in a hawker centre" update
- Wok beat: char kway teow and Hokkien mee now have a rhythm toss, with a clang on every beat
- Mistakes customers notice: the weakest step becomes a specific comment ("a bit greasy, not much wok hei"), and getting a regular's usual wrong costs a heart
- Break-even line before service, and a "where today's money went" coin bar on the closing receipt
- The wet market changes every morning: some ingredients fresh and cheap, some pricey
- Mentor chapters teach real skills: faster cooking, a supplier discount, more patient customers, the recipe, and teaching
- Lunch-hour tables: a table-flow game where you seat diners, clear trays and respect every chope
- Ask the hawker: ask your mentor questions at the stall and learn by talking
- Hawker Moments: honest, kind answers (halal questions, helping an elderly customer with QR payments, a tourist's pronunciation)
- Lived-in detail: ceiling fans, flickering tube lights, a 1989 photo and a handwritten "Today's special" board
